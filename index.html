November 14, 9 AM–6 PM Eastern. Start with a You.com API walkthrough and team matching, spend six hours building with mentors, then demo to the judges. Food is provided throughout the day. Detailed timings will come from the hosts.

New York, NY. The exact address is shared through Luma with registered attendees. Registration requires host approval.

First place overall wins $500 cash. A separate Best Content prize is open to every team; its prize and the judging panel are yet to be announced.
